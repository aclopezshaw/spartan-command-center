# Rival battalion patch prompts

The full prompts used for the built-in generation calls are recorded below. Each call used `transparent_background: true`, Athena as the style/template reference and the originating Fireteam patch as the second reference. Originals are retained and final selected outputs copied into `public/images/command/rivals/`.

## Ares

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: production Spartan Command Center battalion identity patch, a derivative companion variant of the Athena patch in reference image 1. Input images: image 1 is the existing Athena family/style template, not an identity to copy literally; image 2 is the originating rival Fireteam identity reference. Produce exactly ONE new rival battalion patch, isolated on genuinely transparent alpha. Match image 1's tactile black embroidered twill, thick black merrowed stitched border, restrained gunmetal/cold-silver thread and realistic worn military finish. Straight-on orthographic view, square canvas, full patch completely visible with consistent narrow transparent margin, central emblem legible at 76 pixels. The result should complement Athena without being brighter, grander or more ornate. Preserve the recognizable origin palette and motifs of image 2, but evolve them into the specified battalion symbol. No surrounding fabric or olive scenery, no tabletop, no opaque rectangle, no floating external glow, no presentation sheet, no lettering, no words, no numbers, no labels, no laurels, no watermark. All design marks must be inside the embroidered patch. ARES TRAINING BATTALION / Fireteam Sigma / Physical. Black angular clipped-shoulder shield with a pointed base. Dominant silver gunmetal forward-facing Spartan war helmet with a restrained oxblood crimson crest, backed by two crossed short military blades. Deep burgundy/black shield field with subtle tactical map-grid stitching and five small crimson command nodes recalling Sigma's plotted assault points. Strong compact aggressive silhouette, grounded and disciplined. Accent palette oxblood #8F3F48, charcoal #080B0D, weathered gunmetal and cold silver; red is a restrained thread accent rather than a glowing field. Do not reproduce Athena's cobalt round shield or five converging spears.
