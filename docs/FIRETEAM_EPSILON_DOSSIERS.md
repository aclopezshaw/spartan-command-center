## Shared presentation standard

- Portraits use a vertical 1024 × 1536 frame, three-quarter body crop, charcoal operations backdrop, soft key light, and restrained cobalt rim light.
- Every member wears the approved SCP Tactical Field Uniform from `public/images/uniforms/scp-tactical-field-uniform-guide.png`.
- The SCP institutional emblem is worn on the wearer's left upper sleeve.
- The Fireteam Epsilon patch is worn on the wearer's right upper sleeve.
- The portraits contain no baked-in names, ranks, interface labels, weapons, armor, medals, or mission-specific equipment.
- Earlier explorations may remain as versioned files, but unversioned filenames are canonical.

## Event-image continuity

- Event scenes are viewed through ALEX-225's first-person perspective; ALEX-225 does not appear as an external fifth teammate.
- No more than Michael, Ellie, Paige, and Zoe may appear as members of Fireteam Epsilon in one scene.
- Other candidates may appear only when the event explicitly includes competing Fireteams.
- Faces, builds, hair, uniforms, and insignia follow the canonical dossier assets.
- Task-issued equipment may be added without replacing or redesigning the base Tactical Field Uniform.
- Every firearm-shaped training weapon is completely matte safety blue so it cannot be mistaken for a live-fire weapon; communications equipment and other non-weapon gear remain black.
- First-person and teammate weapon orientation must preserve safe muzzle direction and must never flag another Fireteam Epsilon member.
- Cover is evaluated from the opposing threat's perspective: personnel remain on the protected near side of a barrier, maintain safe-ready grips, and orient toward ALEX-225 when receiving a hand signal.
